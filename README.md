4. Consumer complaints and company responses
Main question. Which issues appear most often in consumer complaints, and how do
response outcomes differ across complaint categories?
Data source. CFPB: Consumer Complaint Database. Each row is a published complaint.
Filter to one completed year and one or two products. Read the field descriptions.
Possible EDA directions:
• Compare common issues and company response categories within your selected
products. Use structured fields; text analysis is not necessary.
• Examine response proportions by issue or submission channel. Report denomina-
tors and avoid strong conclusions from very small groups.
• Check missing fields, inconsistent categories, and rare outcomes. Record your filters
and download date.
Optional modeling. Classify the response category from product, issue, and submission
channel using logistic regression, categorical Naive Bayes, or a decision tree. Exclude other
outcome fields from the predictors.
Important caution. Complaints are not representative of all customers. Complaint counts
alone cannot establish which company is worse: company size, market share, and report-
ing behavior matter.
